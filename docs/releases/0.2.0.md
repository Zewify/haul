- Video from most sites, not just Facebook: HLS and DASH streams from any site are saved whole, and video whose sound comes separately (TED, Instagram, Bilibili and others) is joined into one MP4 with its sound.
- The video you are watching is listed first in the popup, marked Playing now, instead of an ad or a neighbouring video in a feed. The sites it was tested on are behind the new ? in the toolbar.
- Protected (DRM) and live streams are refused in a plain sentence instead of saving something that will not play, and YouTube pages say it is not supported.
- A speed limit for all downloads together, in Settings → Connections and in the menu bar menu.
- Haul checks once a day for a newer release and offers the install command in its menu; Settings → General turns this off.
- Settings… in the menu bar menu opens Settings again, file names from long or slashed page titles come out whole, and the download folder reads ~/Downloads.

# Haul

A download manager for the Mac, by Zewify.
Haul takes the downloads your browser starts and fetches them over several connections at once, pauses and resumes them, and keeps them in a queue.

- **Fast.** Each file is split into segments that download in parallel, and idle connections take over the tail of busy ones.
- **Resumable.** Pause, lose the connection, or quit: Haul carries on from where it stopped.
- **In your browser.** Extensions for Safari and for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and Vivaldi hand downloads over as you click them, cookies included, so files behind a login just work.
- **Private.** Haul talks to its extension on your own Mac only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Free, with a one-time Pro unlock for more connections and more downloads at a time.
The direct edition from zewify.com can also save videos and HLS streams from pages.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the layout and the commands.
